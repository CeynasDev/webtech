Ben 10

Ben Tennyson’s life changed forever during a childhood camping trip when he unexpectedly discovered the alien device known as the Omnitrix. While transforming into powerful extraterrestrial creatures brings an incredible rush, it also carries the heavy burden of protecting the entire planet from dangerous cosmic threats. Balancing these sudden intergalactic battles with the everyday struggles of being a normal teenager makes keeping up with high school nearly impossible. Unexpected glitches in the device frequently force him to adapt on the fly, testing his instincts during high-stakes fights when things don't go according to plan. Ultimately, Ben learns that being a true hero relies on inner courage and personal responsibility rather than just relying on the watch on his wrist.
